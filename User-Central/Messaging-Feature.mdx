The **Messaging** section in User Central is your direct line to the **VoxDash support team** (the platform administrators). Every conversation you start here goes to VoxDash, not to a data provider.

<Note>
  Messaging is **not** used to talk to data providers. To contact a data provider about a dataset you requested, use the chat built into that request on the **Request Access** page. See [Communicate with the Provider](/Access-Controls/Requesting-Access).
</Note>

## 1. What You Can Use Messaging For

Contact the VoxDash support team when you want to:

* Ask a question about your account or subscription
* Report a problem with the platform
* Ask for help using a feature
* Send feedback or a suggestion
* Raise any other question for the VoxDash team

All conversations are stored within the platform, so you can come back to them later.

## 2. Start a New Conversation

1. Open **User Central → Messaging**.
2. Click the **+** button next to the search box.
3. Enter a **Subject** (up to 100 characters) and your **Message** (up to 500 characters).
4. Optionally, attach a file, such as a screenshot of the problem.
5. Click **Submit**.

The new conversation opens in the list, and the VoxDash support team receives your message.

<Tip>
  The subject and message cannot contain these characters: `; " # \ < > & % + | ^ $ * ? @` and the backtick. The form shows which characters to remove before you submit.
</Tip>

## 3. Manage Your Conversations

* Select a conversation in the list to open it and read the replies.
* A badge on a conversation shows how many new messages it has.
* Use the search box to find a conversation by its subject.
* Reply at the bottom of an open conversation to continue it.

## 4. Messaging vs. Access Request Chat

| | **Messaging** | **Access request chat** |
|---|---|---|
| Who you talk to | The VoxDash support team | The data provider that owns the dataset |
| Where to find it | User Central → **Messaging** | User Central → **Request Access**, inside each request |
| Use it for | Platform, account, and general questions | Your use case, the provider's questions, and access conditions for that request |

For more about access requests, see [Requesting Access to Restricted Data](/Access-Controls/Requesting-Access) and [Request Access Management](/User-Central/Access-Management).
