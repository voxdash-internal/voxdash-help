The **AI Assistant** works inside the Questionnaire Editor. You can ask it about a question, a page, chosen sections, or the whole questionnaire, and it replies with **suggestions** that you review before anything changes.

<Note>
  AI requests use your organization's **AI credits**. The estimated cost is shown before you send a request. See [AI Credits](/AI-&-Analysis/AI-credits).
</Note>

## Open the AI Assistant

You can open it in two ways:

- Select the **AI** icon (sparkles) at the bottom of the property panel rail on the right of the editor.
- Select **Ask AI about this question** on a question in the canvas.

## Choose What the AI Works On

The line under the chat tabs shows the current **scope**, for example *This question · Q1*. Select **Change** to pick another scope:

| Scope | What the AI looks at |
| --- | --- |
| **This question** | The question selected on the canvas. |
| **This page** | All questions on the current page. |
| **Choose sections…** | One or more sections you pick. |
| **Whole questionnaire** | Every question in the questionnaire. |

When you have applied filters in the editor, you can also work on the **filtered questions** only.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/QB/d-ai-scope.png" alt="Scope menu of the AI Assistant" style={{ borderRadius: '0.5rem', maxWidth: '520px' }} />
</Frame>

<Tip>
  In a new chat, selecting something on the canvas switches the scope to it automatically. Once a request is running, the scope of that chat is fixed.
</Tip>

## Ask the AI

Type your request in the box at the bottom — for example *"Make this question easier to read aloud"* — or pick a **quick action**:

| Quick action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Suggest question type** | Recommends a better-suited question type for the question. |
| **Check how it sounds** | Checks how the text will sound when read aloud by text-to-speech and flags words that may be mispronounced. |
| **Shorten questions** | Proposes shorter wording to reduce interview length. |
| **Review the questionnaire** | Reviews the questions in scope and flags issues and improvements. |

Other actions may be available depending on your plan, such as suggesting SPSS labels or explaining logic in plain language.

Before you send, the cost button next to **Send** shows the estimated cost of the request.

## Review Suggestions

The AI answers with **suggestion cards**. Each card describes one proposed change or finding, for example *rewrite*, *change type*, *merge*, *remove*, *add*, *flag*, or a *tip*, and is marked **Critical**, **Improvement**, or **Info**.

For each suggestion you can:

- **Accept** — apply the change to the questionnaire. An **Undo** notice appears right after.
- **Reject** — discard the suggestion.
- **Dismiss** — hide a finding without changing anything.
- **Don't flag again** — stop the AI raising the same finding on this question.
- **Show on canvas** — highlight the affected question.
- **Preview** — see how the canvas would look with the change, and open the preview in a tab.

When a request returns many suggestions, use review mode: move with **Next** and **Previous**, or use **Accept all** / **Reject all**. Keyboard shortcuts are listed in the panel.

<Note>
  If the questionnaire changed after the AI made a suggestion, the card may be marked **outdated**, or say that the question is missing or the list changed. You can still choose **Apply anyway** where that is safe. Suggestions that save interview time show the estimated seconds saved.
</Note>

### Pronunciation Check

**Check how it sounds** returns a card that lists words the voice may mispronounce. For each word you can:

- **Say as…** — enter how the word should be spoken,
- **Say in another language** — have the word pronounced in another language,
- **Save** — store the correction as a custom pronunciation for text-to-speech.

Saved corrections appear in the [Text-to-Speech](/Questionnaire-Builder/tts) panel.

## Runs, Progress, and Cost

Large requests run in steps. While a request runs, the chat shows its progress, and you can **Stop** it. When a request ends early or only partly succeeds, the chat says so and offers **Try again** or **Redo**.

You are **charged only for what was processed**. The final cost appears in the chat when the run finishes.

## Chats and History

- Each request lives in a **chat tab**. Select **+** to start a new chat, or **×** to close one. There is a maximum number of open chats; close one to start another.
- Select the **history** icon to see earlier chats on this questionnaire. Filter by scope (**All**, **Question**, **Section**, **Selection**, **Questionnaire**), by action, or show only chats that **Need review**.

## When the AI Is Unavailable

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Not included in your plan | Your subscription doesn't include AI actions. Select **Upgrade** to see plans. |
| Plan limit reached | Your plan's limit for this questionnaire has been reached. Select **Upgrade**. |
| Not enough AI credits | The organization's credit balance can't cover the estimated cost. Top up AI credits. |
| Locked or read-only version | AI changes can't be accepted on a locked, published, or previewed version. Create or switch to an editable version. |
| Action unavailable | The action is temporarily unavailable or not supported for the selected scope. |

## Readable Logic for Exports

The **Excel data map** export can include plain-language descriptions of your logic. When you configure that export, a panel shows how many questions already have a readable description and lets you have the AI write the missing ones. See [Export](/Questionnaire-Builder/Export).
