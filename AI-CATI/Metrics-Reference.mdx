---
title: "Interview Metrics: Reference"
description: "Every AI-CATI interview metric with its dictionary ID, unit and range, whether the Standard and Live pipelines measure it, and where it is shown."
---

The IDs (M1.1 to M10.2) follow the AI-CATI implicit measurement metric dictionary. **Standard** and **Live** say whether each voice pipeline measures the metric: **Yes**, **Partly** (measured, with the limit in the note), or **No**.

Where a metric is shown: **Q** the Call quality card, **B** the Respondent behaviour card, **A** the admin Call audio and timing panel, **R** the Result tab's per-question rows, **D** the dashboards, **S** stored with the call's analysis only.
